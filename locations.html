Before you go

Tips for a smooth visit

A few quick steps help keep your vehicle — and our tunnel — safe and clean.

  • Know how to put your car in neutral before entering the tunnel.
  • Fold mirrors in if needed.
  • Remove antenna accessories when required.
  • Take off the hitch before the wash.
  • Remove any items from the bed of your truck.
  • Follow instructions from the attendant for a safe and clean experience.
