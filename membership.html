FAQ

Common questions

What is the Unlimited Wash Club?

A monthly membership that lets you wash your vehicle as often as you need at the wash level included in your plan — for one flat monthly rate.

Where can I sign up?

Sign up on-site at either Picayune location (kiosk or with our team). Call (601) 798-6331 if you have questions first.

Is membership good at both locations?

We intend club members to enjoy both Hwy 43 South and Hayden Oaks — please confirm with staff when you join.

How often do I get billed?

You get charged once a month and on the same day of every month.

Can I share my membership?

Most unlimited clubs are one vehicle per membership. Ask about your specific plan terms when you enroll.

How do I cancel or update billing?

Contact the wash at (601) 798-6331 for billing changes, plate updates, or cancellation. Final policy is set by the business.

Ready to join the club?

Stop by either location or call — we’ll help you pick the right plan.
